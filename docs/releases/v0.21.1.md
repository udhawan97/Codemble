# Codemble v0.21.1 — choose a launch, land, and learn

v0.21.1 is the verified publication of Codemble's learn-as-you-explore galaxy.
It carries the complete v0.21.0 feature candidate and one release-integrity
repair: source archives now exclude the developer-only `uv.lock` that
`uv run --with build` can create before Hatchling starts.

The trusted v0.21.0 workflow caught that local/clean-checkout mismatch before
PyPI publication. Its immutable tag remains a blocked prerelease; no v0.21.0
package was uploaded or replaced.

## Choose how to enter

- **Explore freely** opens the complete galaxy immediately.
- **Take a first flight** tours Home and its direct proven imports, with a
  visible **Land and learn** action at every stop.
- Every landing offers persistent Easy or Expert explanations, exact
  parser-owned source facts and graph connections, and the existing
  **Prove understanding** route.

## A deeper galaxy

- Seeded spiral stars, depth shells, nebulae, dust, a galactic core, route
  motes, and reticles make each project feel like its own navigable place.
- Planet surfaces add deterministic terrain, mineral bands, atmosphere, tilt,
  and slow rotation while preserving reduced motion and recursive disposal.
- All semantic colour remains evidence-bounded: language tint mirrors parser
  truth, while amber illumination still means passed checks only.

## Nine-language mapping

Ruby and PHP join Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Go, Java, Rust, and C# behind
the same deterministic parser/finalization seam. The new adapters preserve PHP
qualification and grouped imports, Ruby singleton ownership and conservative
bare sends, safe partial-file fallback, and possible-call uncertainty.
