# Codemble v0.21.0 — choose a launch, land, and learn

v0.21.0 turns the explorable galaxy into a clearer adventure without turning
scenery into evidence. A first run can open the whole sky or begin a bounded
First Flight, every structure has an Easy/Expert landing brief, the procedural
space and world art is materially deeper, and Ruby plus PHP bring the parser
slate to nine languages.

## Highlights

- **Choose your launch:** Explore freely dismisses onboarding and opens the
  complete Galaxy. Take a first flight persists Easy or Expert, then follows the
  bounded tour of Home and the modules it directly imports. **Land and learn**
  at any stop opens a real parser-owned structure, then the existing **Prove
  understanding** action continues into graph-derived checks.
- **Land and learn:** a selected world names the real structure, source span,
  parser summary, and inbound/outbound connections. Easy and Expert can switch
  in place before opening source, Impact, the full journey, or checks.
- **A richer sky:** a deterministic spiral disc, layered star shells, dust,
  core, nebula variants, route particles, reticle, and vignette create depth at
  Galaxy range. System worlds add seeded terrain, mineral bands, atmosphere,
  tilt, and slow rotation.
- **Responsive at scale:** projects above 900 source files yield for 650 ms
  before constructing the 3D sky, name that preparation honestly, and cancel
  it if the complete Canvas Map takes over. The 5,000-module gate reached a
  usable app in 1.48 s in Chromium and 2.03 s in WebKit.
- **Landing-first on small screens:** the structure explanation stays in the
  first compact viewport. Exact inbound, outbound, and possible-connection
  counts provide the continuation cue; the complete journey remains reachable
  without hiding source or check actions.
- **Ruby and PHP:** official tree-sitter grammar wheels feed independent,
  conservative adapters with imports/includes, declarations, calls,
  entrypoints, syntax-backed Lens concepts, partial-file safety, and mixed-
  project composition. Ruby also emits native entrypoint role evidence; PHP
  intentionally emits none.

## Correctness boundaries

- Launch choice changes navigation and teaching depth only. The graph, layout,
  source, checks, visits, and understood state stay identical.
- The camera remains on rails. There is no free translation, XP, score, streak,
  level, leaderboard, saved voyage, or new quest type.
- Decorative layers add no unsupported fact. Language tint mirrors the parsed
  language, and amber/starbursts mirror check-owned understanding; possible
  routes remain dashed and still. Scenery never invents role, certainty, or
  importance.
- Ruby and PHP dynamic dispatch stays labelled possible unless explicit syntax
  identifies the target. A syntax-error file retains safe module evidence only.
- PHP preserves fully qualified, namespace-relative, aliased, and grouped-use
  names. Ruby preserves `class << self` ownership and records standalone
  no-argument sends as possible unless local binding syntax rules them out.
- Codemble still asks for a smaller scope above 5,000 supported files. The
  complete Canvas Map remains the no-WebGL fallback.
