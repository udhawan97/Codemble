# v0.9.0 — the loop holds, and the chrome gets out of its way

Released 2026-08-01 · tag `v0.9.0`

Everything that accumulated since v0.8.0, closed by an evidence-led user-flow
audit of the served build. Two of the findings are worth stating plainly,
because both had already survived the CI job that exists to catch them.

## Highlights

- **Clearing your progress no longer breaks the loop.** "Clear this project's
  progress" dimmed every module but kept the individual questions marked as
  answered, so reopening the quiz on a module you had already proved showed a
  panel containing a title and a Close button and nothing else — no question,
  no way forward, and no route back to lighting it short of restarting the
  server. Illumination is the whole game, and the app's own recovery control
  turned it off permanently for every module proved in that session. The reset
  now forgets both halves of that state.
- **The header holds one row at every desktop width and every level.** Between
  1024px and 1279px the row of actions wrapped as soon as you opened a module,
  and again when you opened its source: 148px → 199px → **259px**, 52% of a
  720px window, with the Architecture drawing squeezed to 190px. The buttons
  now hold their row and the brand gives way instead. At 1024 the drawing gets
  **301px** back, up from 190.
- **Your folder's name no longer sets the header's height.** The brand's second
  line is the project directory you pointed Codemble at, and it was sizing the
  rail: a 60-character name reproduced the same wrap at 1440px. That is a
  variable the product does not control, and it no longer participates.
- **A relationship Codemble could not prove is now dashed in the galaxy too,**
  not merely a different shade — the one claim a learner must never read as
  fact now carries a shape as well as a colour, on both layers.
- **Two unrelated parts of your project no longer wear the same colour.** Hue
  means "which part of the project is this", and with thirty-nine communities
  the old arithmetic put five distinct ones on the same family. The graph now
  names the project's eight largest and leaves the rest on the neutral ramp —
  an honest absence beats a false claim.
- **The galaxy opens on your whole charted sky,** aimed at what it is framing
  rather than at the origin, and it is no longer 98.7% empty space.
- **The public site can be read without browser zoom,** at an 18px baseline
  with product captures at a size where the app inside them is legible.

The rest — Fit showing more of the diagram instead of less, Escape closing what
is open and only what is open, focus returning immediately rather than up to
four seconds later, the quiz keeping its identifiers whole and its button on
screen — is in the changelog.

## What this release also changed about how it is checked

Both headline gaps passed the existing browser gate, and for structural reasons
rather than luck:

- it measured only the **top** level, where the rail carries one action fewer
  than the loop a learner actually spends time in, so the wrap began one click
  below anything asserted;
- it runs against a checkout directory named `Codemble` — eight characters,
  where the row fits. The bug needs a real learner's folder name.

The space budget now walks region and study level at four wide widths, fails
when two header controls overlap, and injects a 70-character project name. The
Escape gate was reaching nothing it claimed to: it clicked a control named
"Map" while the Easy register renames it "Diagram", so eight of its 84
assertions had been failing for the wrong reason instead of exercising the
checks panel and the map retreat.

## Known limits

- **The wordmark clips by 2px at 1024–1059px, at study level only.** Accepted
  rather than fixed: the alternatives were truncating the breadcrumb — which
  answers "where am I" and is navigation, not identity — or demoting a 36px
  band of window widths to the compact shell.
- **Phase 1 tester evidence is still open.** Issue #13 asks for unaided runs by
  real learners on their own projects; technical completion does not stand in
  for it, and this release does not claim it.
- **Measured on macOS with Chrome.** The layout findings here are text-width
  sensitive by nature, so Firefox and Safari may differ in detail.
- **One project.** Everything was measured against Codemble itself (1224 nodes,
  125 systems, 7616 edges). A much larger or much smaller project may frame
  differently.
- **Themes, touch hardware, and LLM narration were not exercised.** The app
  ships one dark theme and no toggle; compact widths were driven with an
  emulated pointer; no API key was configured, so study explanations were seen
  only in their no-key state.
